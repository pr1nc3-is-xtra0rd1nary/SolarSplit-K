Safety first

Responsible research

Required safety measures

  • Gas-production experiments require an approved risk assessment before the work begins.
  • Only trained supervision is acceptable, especially if students are working with reactive materials or a light source.
  • Use suitable apparatus, correct venting arrangements, and a qualified laboratory when required.
  • Never work alone on gas-producing chemistry or unfamiliar photocatalytic systems.

Important hazard warning

Do not use table salt as an electrolyte in systems intended for electrolysis. In an electrolysis system, chloride from table salt can produce hazardous chlorine gas and other corrosive products. This project is about safe, evidence-based photocatalysis; it is not an instruction for DIY gas generation or storage.

What this project does not include

  • No instructions for assembling gas collection equipment.
  • No gas storage, compression, or pressurization steps.
  • No ignition, flammable gas handling, or combustion demonstrations.
  • No claim that a bubble is proof of water splitting until a qualified method verifies hydrogen and oxygen.

Safety is part of the science. Treat it seriously.
