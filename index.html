Project overview

Purpose

Investigate low-cost photocatalytic water splitting for a science fair project, while learning how to compare experiments fairly and keep evidence honest.

Important scientific rule

Bubbles alone do not prove complete water splitting. A proper claim requires reliable identification and quantitative verification of both hydrogen and oxygen by a qualified laboratory or suitable instrumentation.

Real objective

Use the literature and experiments to identify one specific, testable improvement rather than claiming a novelty before the evidence supports it.
